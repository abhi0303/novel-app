At four o'clock the light goes, all at once, as if somebody had pulled a blind over the sky. The second band of the storm hits the island like a fist.

Nobody wants to be alone, and nobody wants to be together. They drift to their rooms one by one. Doors lock up and down the house.

At the end of the corridor, Farida pauses at her door and looks back at you for a long moment. Then she goes in, and you hear her key turn.
