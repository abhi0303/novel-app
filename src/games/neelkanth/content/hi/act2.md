@@ a2-scream

**Ravivaar, 14 June. Subah saadhe chhe baje.**

Poori tarah jaagne se pehle hi aap bistar se nikal kar daud {{rahe|rahi}} hain. Cheekh verandah ke aakhri chhor se aayi thi.

Tara study ke darwaaze par khadi hain, chai ki tray unke pairon mein toot kar bikhri hai, dono haath munh par.

Aapke Kaka khidki ke neeche diwan par pade hain, karvat liye, ek haath neeche latka hua. Ab bhi kal wala kurta pehne hain. Unke paas zameen par ek basin hai, aur ek baalti, aur kamre mein khatti gandh hai.

Aapne bahut saare mare hue log dekhe hain. Chhoone se pehle hi aapko pata hai.

Peechhe poore ghar mein darwaaze khul rahe hain. Kadam. Nandini ki aawaaz: "Kya hua? Kya ho gaya?" Sid, aadhe kapdon mein. Commander, langdaate hue, shirt pehente hue. Farida, sabse aakhir mein, safed dressing gown mein, bahut zard.

Bahar toofan ruka nahi hai. Balki aur bura hai. Khidki ke paar samundar ek ubalta hua safed maidaan hai, aur lehrein jetty ke sire ke upar se toot rahi hain.

Aaj koi naav nahi aayegi.

@@ a2-study

Woh darwaaze par ruk jaate hain. Ek pal ke liye, kyunki aapne yeh waise hi kaha jaise ward mein {{kehte|kehti}} hain, woh maan jaate hain.

@@ h-body

Aap diwan ke paas ghutnon par {{baithte|baithti}} hain.

Woh thande hain, aur poore akde hue: jabde, baahon aur pairon mein rigor mortis poora hai. Khoon us taraf jam gaya hai jis karvat woh lete hain, gehra baingani, jo dabaane par safed nahi hota. Dono milaakar kehte hain ki unhe mare chaar-paanch ghante ho gaye. Raat do baje, shaayad dhaai. Zyaada se zyaada teen.

Raat mein unhe kai baar ulti hui: basin, baalti, farsh. Gardan par koi nishaan nahi, aankhon ke safed hisse mein koi khoon ke dhabbe nahi, baahon par koi neel nahi. Kisi ne unke munh par takiya nahi dabaaya. Honthon par halka neela rang hai.

Aap {{sochte|sochti}} hain ki aap kya {{jaante|jaanti}} hain. Dil ke mareez. Ji machlaana, ulti, phir dil jo dheema hota hai, ladkhadaata hai aur ruk jaata hai. Yeh textbook mein ek hi cheez ki tasveer hai: dil ki dawa digoxin ka overdose. Woh digoxin le rahe the.

Lekin yeh ek tasveer hai, diagnosis nahi. Aapko jaanna hoga ki unhone kya liya.

@@ h-pills

Plastic ka goliyon ka dabba, saat din aur din mein chaar baar ke khaane. Ravivaar subah wala khaana abhi bhara hai. Shanivaar raat 10:30 ka khaana khaali hai: unhone raat ki goliyan li thin.

Bagal mein digoxin ki strip, 0.25 milligram. Kisi ne foil par marker se likha hai: *1 June se shuru*. Din mein ek. Terah din, terah goliyan khatam. Satrah bachi hain.

Bilkul sahi. Unhone apni goliyon ka extra dose nahi liya, aur kisi ne unhe mutthi bhar goliyan nahi khilaayin. Jisne bhi unka dil dheema kiya, woh is strip ka digoxin nahi tha.

@@ h-door
