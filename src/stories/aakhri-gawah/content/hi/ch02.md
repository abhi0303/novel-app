"Ek chor jo is ghar ko lootne aata hai," usne kaha, "mare hue aadmi ki kalai par Rolex chhod kar chala jaata hai. Chaar hazaar wala wallet chhod deta hai. Saamne desk par rakhe sone ke cufflinks chhod deta hai. Chhe mein se ek daraaz kholta hai, aur ek lifafa uthata hai. Woh jis par kisi ka naam likha hai. Ya toh use theek-theek pata tha ki use kya chahiye, Kabir, ya woh chor tha hi nahi."

"Aur tasveer?"

Dev bahut der chup raha.

"Chor tasveerein nahi palat-te," usne aakhir mein kaha. "Log palat-te hain."

Usne haath badhaya, aur is baar frame ko nahi chhua. Bas uske upar haath rakhe raha, jaise Nandita Sethi ne rakha tha, aur aas-paas ke kamre ki thand mehsoos ki. Aaj raat jo bhi is study mein aaya tha, use na Rolex se darr laga tha, na camera se, na ek mare hue aadmi ki khuli aankhon se.

Use darr laga tha ki ek chhoti bachchi ki tasveer dekh legi ki usne kya kiya.
