Phir woh akad kar utha, barsati mein gaya, aur apna kaala school backpack lekar lauta. Woh chain ke paas tile par ghutnon ke bal baitha, saamne ki jeb ki zip kholi, kuch nikaala aur andhere mein Dev ki taraf badhaya.

Ek safed lifafa, beech se muda hua, kone saath rakhne se naram. Dev ne liya aur neeche aangan se aati halki roshni ki taraf ghumaya. Seal band tha. Saamne, ek chaukor, sambhli, pehchaani hui likhawat mein, neeli syahi se:

*Mohan — Dec*

"Maine kabhi khola nahi," Rahul ne kaha. "Sab usmein hai. Mohan bhaiya ka hai. Please unhe de dijiyega. Main… main ise dekh bhi nahi pata tha."

Dev ne lifafa pakda. Uska wazan lagbhag kuch nahi tha, aur usne zindagi mein isse bhaari cheez kabhi nahi pakdi thi.

"Rahul," usne kaha. "Jab tum baad mein nadi ke paas seedhiyon par baithe the. Tumne kya mehsoos kiya?"

Ladke ne gambhirta se socha, jaise kabhi exam ke sawaalon par sochta hoga.

"Socha tha shaanti mehsoos karunga," usne aakhir mein kaha. "Socha tha dus saal se ek shor hai, aur ab ruk jaayega. Yahi socha tha. Ki sab shaant ho jaayega." Usne tile par padi chain ko dekha. "Nahi hua. Kuch shaant nahi hua. Ab bas ek alag shor hai. Doosri baar wala. Aur doorbell. Baar-baar." Usne thook nigla. "Main behtar mehsoos nahi karta. Nahi lagta ki uske liye kuch kiya. Sochta rehta hoon ki use yeh bilkul pasand nahi aata. Woh mujhse itni naraz hoti. Mera kaan kaat deti."

Usne hansne ki koshish ki aur nahi hans paaya.

"Main nahi kahunga ki maine sahi kiya," usne kaha. "Nahi kahunga ki woh iske laayak the, chahe the bhi. Pari yeh nahi karti. Pari ne zindagi mein kabhi kisi ko chot nahi pahunchayi. Yeh maine kiya. Uske liye nahi. Usne mujhse nahi kaha tha. Maine kiya." Usne Dev ko dekha, aur andhere mein uska chehra ek saath bahut jawaan aur bahut boodha tha. "Aur Nandita aunty jail mein hain. Aur Mohan bhaiya ki tankhwah mere bag mein hai. Aur sab samajhte hain ki kisi aur ne kiya."

Woh der tak chup raha.

"Aap mujhe abhi le jaayenge?" usne kaha.

---

Dev ne jawab nahi diya.

Woh thand mein andheri chhat par khada tha. Ek haath mein mare hue aadmi ka lifafa, doosre mein topi, pairon ke paas chain, aur saamne ghutnon ke bal ek ladka jo intezaar kar raha tha ki use bataya jaaye ki uski baaki zindagi ka kya hoga.

Use pata tha kanoon kya kehta hai. Apne baap ko maarne ka kabool karne wala satrah saal ka ladka Juvenile Justice Board ke saamne laaya jaayega. Itne gambhir apraadh mein ek preliminary assessment hoga, aur Board tay kar sakta hai ki uska mukadma ek vayask ki tarah chale. Bachchon ki adaalat hogi, lambi kaarvaai, vakil, saza. Akhbaar honge. *SAMAJ RATNA KO APNE HI BETE NE MAARA.* Rajiv Sethi ke dost Rotary Club, Chamber of Commerce, St. Aloysius ke principal, do MLA aur Police Commissioner the. Woh apne achhe suit pehen kar adaalaton mein baithenge aur zor-zor se yaad karenge ki woh kitne samarpit pita the. Aur us sab mein kahin, ek ladki jo teacher banna chahti thi, zyada se zyada ek 'mitigating circumstance' ban jaayegi.

Use pata tha ki ek aurat aaj raat Lucknow District Jail mein ek chataai par ek aisi cheez ke liye leti hai jo usne nahi ki, kyunki woh kisi ko nahi batayegi ki usne sadsath minute kuch nahi karte hue bitaye. Use pata tha ki uska partner is gali ke aakhir mein engine band kiye gaadi mein uska intezaar kar raha hai, Budhvaar se teen baar keh chuka hai ki yeh theek se karo, aur teen baar use vaada mila hai.

Aur use pata tha, halaanki woh jaanna nahi chahta tha, ki paintees saal pehle Saharanpur mein kisi Ravivaar poori aasteenon mein ek ladka bistar par leta bagal ke kamre mein belt utarne ki aawaaz sunta tha, har raat sochta tha *kaise*, aur subah uth kar school chala jaata tha.

Neeche aangan mein ek batti jali. Sharda Devi ki rasoi. Use counter par steel ka gilaas rakhe jaane ki khanak sunai di.

Gali ke aakhir mein headlights ka ek joda jala, aur phir bujh gaya.

Dev ne ladke ko dekha. Usne isse kam par logon ko giraftaar kiya tha. Isse kahin kam par logon ko jail bheja tha, aur baad mein so gaya tha. Bees saal mein use kabhi itni shiddat se yeh chaah nahi hui thi ki woh na jaane jo jaanta tha.

"Rahul," usne kaha, aur ruk gaya, kyunki use nahi pata tha aage kya hai.

Use nahi pata tha uska jawab kya hai. Sirf itna pata tha ki ab jo bhi kahega, kisi ko saari zindagi uske saath jeena padega. Aur apne career mein pehli baar use bilkul yakeen nahi tha ki woh insaan khud woh nahi hona chahiye.
