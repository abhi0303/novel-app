She was in her fifties, grey-haired, with reading glasses on a beaded chain. Rahul's bedroom at the Vipul Khand house was on the first floor at the back, above the study, and it did not look like the room of a seventeen-year-old boy.

There were no posters. No clothes on the floor, no chargers tangled on the bedside table, no cricket bat leaning in a corner. The bed was made with a tightness that would have pleased an army barracks. The books on the shelf were arranged by subject and then by height. On the wall above the desk was a laminated sheet, typed and printed, with handwritten additions in red ink in a square, careful hand that Dev had already seen on an envelope label:

*5:30 — wake. 6:00–8:00 — self-study (Maths/Physics). 8:30 — school. 4:30 — tuition. 6:30–9:30 — self-study. 10:00 — lights off.*
