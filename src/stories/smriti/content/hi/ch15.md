Usne use khud par band kiya tha, andar se, jaise use kaha gaya tha, ek aisi ladki ne jo toofan mein ek aisi torch lekar uske peechhe aayi thi jise ek aisi gehraai ke upar pakadne se darti thi jisse darti thi, jisne tooti deewar par use ghuma kar ghar ki taraf ishaara kiya tha aur raasta roshan karne ke liye bahari kinare par khadi ho gayi thi, aur kaha tha, *andar jao, darwaza band karna*. Aur jab pahad gaya, tab bhi wahin khadi thi, roshni pakde.

---

Darwaze ki doosri taraf koi khatkhata raha tha. Dheere, ek ungli ke jod se, sheeshe par.

Usne aankhen kholin aur bar dhakela, aur darwaza bahar jhoola, aur Kabir baarish mein landing par tha, neeche se aati torch ki roshni mein uska chehra geela.

"Maine aapko band karte suna," usne kaha. "Seedhi se. Bar suna." Woh ruka. "Meera."

"Woh mujhe chhod kar nahi gayi," Meera ne kaha.

Usne apni aawaaz ko ye kehte suna, bilkul shaant, darwaze mein, peechhe se guzarti baarish ke saath. Phir use dobara suna, jaise kahin aur se, jaise ye kisi case file ka ek vaakya ho jo woh bahut der se padh rahi thi aur ab jaakar samjhi ho.

"Mujhe laga main dhoondh rahi hoon woh kahan gayi," usne kaha. "Poore hafte. Mujhe laga main Ananya ko dhoondh rahi hoon." Usne uske paar pahadi raaste ko dekha, andhere mein poorab ki taraf jaata peela geela patthar aur hawa mein khatam hota. "Us raat gayab hone wali main thi, Kabir. Woh mujhe dhoondh rahi thi."

Unke neeche, lohe ki seedhi ke neeche, Dev Malik ne apni torch neeche kar li thi. Geele chabootre se upar aati halki roshni mein Meera ko uska chehra unki taraf mudaa dikha, aur usne use samajhte dekha. Saat saal pehle woh isi jagah khada tha aur safed kapdon wali ek aurat ko laal darwaza band karte dekha tha, aur saat saal yakeen kiya tha ki woh kisi ko bahar band kar rahi hai.

Woh ghar laut rahi thi.
