Vikram Sethi sirf ek businessman nahi tha.

Dehradun, Delhi aur Mussoorie mein uske teen hotel the. Do shehron mein zameen, ek construction company mein hissa, aur charity ke naam par ek trust jiske board mein aadha shehar baitha tha. Akhbaar use "Doon ka self-made aadmi" kehte the. Tees saal pehle woh ek chhota sa contractor tha. Aaj uske naam se bank ke manager khade ho jaate the.

Uski zindagi mein paisa bahut tha. Lekin bharosa, shayad bahut kam.

Hotel ki library ko interview room bana diya gaya. Kitaabon ki almariyan, hare kapde wali ek lambi table, aur kone mein ek purana globe jis par Burma ab bhi British Empire mein tha.

Chaar suspects se pehle Dev ne Neelam ko bulaya.

@scene four-suspects | Chaar log jo us raat Vikram Sethi se jude the.

Murder ki raat Vikram se milne chaar log aaye the. Ya yun kahiye, chaar log us raat uski zindagi ke sabse kareeb the. Aur chaaron ab ek-ek karke hari table ke saamne baithne wale the.

**Arjun Malhotra** gyarah baje pahuncha. Pachaas ke kareeb, mehngi leather jacket jiske kinaare Kolhukhet ke keechad se bhare the. Baal peeche ki taraf set, jaise subah ke dhabe mein bhi usne aaina dhoondh liya ho. Usne baithne se pehle Dev se haath milaya, aur Kabir se nahi.

"Bahut bura hua," usne kaha. "Baarah saal ka saath tha. Jhagde hote the, business mein kahan nahi hote? Lekin yeh..." Usne sir hilaya.

"Kal raat ke baare mein bataiye."

"Dinner tha. Vikram ne bikri ki baat chhedi. Main khush nahi tha, maine bata diya. Phir main 9:45 par hotel se nikal gaya. Seedha Dehradun ke liye. Kolhukhet ke aage sadak band thi, toh ek dhabe mein raat kaati." Usne apni ghadi dekhi, jaise uske paas aur bhi zaroori kaam hon. "Vikram se meri us raat dobara koi mulaqat nahi hui."

"Vikram aur main pichhle kuch hafton se alag kamron mein so rahe the," usne pehle hi kaha, jaise yeh baat baad mein kisi aur se pata chalne se behtar ho ki woh khud bata de. "Woh der raat tak kaam karta tha. Mujhe uski light mein neend nahi aati thi."

"Dinner ke baad main apne kamre mein chali gayi. Main 10 baje ke baad apne kamre mein thi."
