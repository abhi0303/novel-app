December ke pehle hafte mein Mussoorie mein pehli baar aasmaan poori tarah saaf hua.

Lal Tibba se door barfeele pahad dikhne lage. Bandarpoonch, Swargarohini, aur unke peechhe aur bhi, ek safed deewar jaisi jo raat bhar mein kisi ne aasmaan ke kinaare par khadi kar di ho. Mall Road par sailaani laut aaye the. Monkeys laut aaye the. Aur Kulri ke chauraahe par, chai ki ek tapri ke saamne, ek railing par do aadmi kohni tikaye khade the.

Kabir ke haath mein kulhad tha, aur woh use aise pakde hue tha jaise duniya ki aakhri garmi wahi ho.

"Sir," usne kaha. "Aapne kaha tha baad mein bataoge."

Dev ne door pahadon ki taraf dekha.

Pichhle chhe hafton mein bahut kuch hua tha.

Arjun Malhotra Sudhowala jail mein tha, fraud ke trial ke intezaar mein. Uska saala bhi pakda gaya tha. Himgiri Constructions ke account se ₹4.2 crore mein se dhai crore wapas mil gaye the. Baaki Delhi ke us doobe hue project mein kahin gum the. Arjun ne jail se Dev ko ek chhoti si chitthi likhi thi, sirf teen line ki: *Main uski chautha mein nahi ja saka. Agar aap kabhi uski samadhi par jaayein, toh meri taraf se kuch mat kehna. Main us layak nahi hoon.*

Dr. Sameer Kapoor ne khud Medical Council ko likh kar bataya tha ki 14 October ki raat 10:22 par usne kya dekha aur kya nahi kiya. Council ki inquiry abhi chal rahi thi. Lekin us inquiry ke faisle se pehle hi doctor ne apni Dehradun wali clinic band kar di thi. Ab woh Raipur ke paas ek gaon ki charitable dispensary mein hafte mein chhe din baith-ta tha. Muft. Usne kisi ko nahi bataya kyun. Kisi ko batane ki zaroorat bhi nahi thi.

Meera aur doctor ab nahi mile. Kuch rishte andhere mein hi zinda rehte hain. Sach ki roshni mein aate hi unki saans ruk jaati hai.

Vikram ki nayi will kabhi likhi hi nahi gayi. Purani will ke hisaab se sab kuch Meera ka tha. Usne Sharma ji ko bulaya, aur pehla faisla yeh kiya ki Silver Pine nahi bikega. Doosra yeh ki hotel sardiyon ke liye band rahega, aur staff ki tankhwaah poori milegi. Neelam ko, Mohan ko, Pooja ko, Thapa ko, Bahadur ko.

Aur teesra faisla aisa tha jiske baare mein akhbaaron ko kuch nahi bataya gaya. Dehradun ke us sarkari school mein, jahan Sunita Malhotra tees saal pehle bachchon ko padhati thi, ek scholarship shuru hui. *Smt. Sunita Malhotra Smriti Chhatravritti.* Har saal das ladkiyon ke liye.

Meera ek baar Sudhowala bhi gayi thi. Rohan se mili. Kya baat hui, kisi ko nahi pata. Jailor ne baad mein Dev ko sirf itna bataya ki woh aadha ghanta baithi rahi, zyada tar chup. Aur jaate waqt usne Rohan ke haath mein ek purani, kinaaron se ghisi hui photo di. Ek pachchees-chhabbees saal ka ladka, ek purane scooter par baitha, baal hawa mein, aankhon mein bade sapne.

Vikram Sethi. 1994.

Rohan ne photo ko bahut der tak dekha, jailor ne kaha tha. Phir use apni shirt ki jeb mein rakh liya, dil ke paas.

Dev bhi ek baar Sudhowala gaya tha. Court ki ijazat se, chitthi ki ek photocopy lekar.

Rohan ne photocopy li aur padhi. Chupchaap. Do baar. Phir use mod kar apni god mein rakh liya, bahut aaraam se, jaise koi chidiya ho jo ud jaayegi.

"Sir," usne kaha. "Aap Dehradun jaate ho?"

"Haan. Har hafte."

"Rajpur Road ke peechhe, Rispana ke kinaare, ek purana ghaat hai. Chhota sa. Peepal ka ek ped hai. Maa wahan baith-ti thi, shaam ko, school ke baad." Rohan ne apne haath dekhe. "Agle saal chaudah October ko, main shayad yahin rahunga. Aap... aap ek diya jala denge? Wahan?"

"Jala dunga."

Rohan ne kuch der kuch nahi kaha. Phir usne bahut dheere se kaha:

"Do diye, sir."

"Do?"

"Ek maa ke liye." Woh ruka. Bahut der tak. Uski ungliyan photocopy ke kinaare ko chhoo rahi thin, theek us jagah jahan asli panne par ek patti phat kar alag hui thi. "Aur ek uske liye. Usne likha tha ki woh jalana chahta tha. Do saal se nahi jala paaya." Usne Dev ki taraf dekha. "Kisi ko toh jalana chahiye, sir."

Dev ne sir hilaya.

"Do diye," usne kaha.

"Toh, sir?" Kabir ne kulhad ki aakhri ghoont li. "Kab pata chala?"

Dev ne elaichi nikaali. Is baar khaayi.

"Basement mein. Doosre din. Jab woh darwaze tak gaya, ruka, mud kar aaya, aur bina kisi ke poochhe bola ki usne doctor ko bottle ke saath dekha tha." Dev ne pahadon ki taraf dekha. "Kisi ne nahi poochha tha, Kabir. Jo insaan bina poochhe madad karta hai, woh kabhi-kabhi apni kahani ki madad kar raha hota hai."

"Doosre din?" Kabir ne kulhad neeche rakh diya. "Aap aath din chup rahe?"

"Tab woh sirf ek shak tha. Ek ajeeb sa ehsaas. Ki ek aadmi jo itna shaant hai, itna sahi hai, itna madadgaar hai, woh ek aisi baat kyun bol raha hai jo kisi ne poochhi hi nahi." Dev ne kandhe uchkaye. "Shak ko saboot banne mein ek hafta laga. Jab Norbu ke camera ne dikhaya ki bottle 10:15 tak gaadi mein thi, tab us shak ne pehli baar naam liya. Aur jab doctor ne dark blazer ki baat ki, tab woh naam pakka hua. Baaki sab, generator, ghadi, diesel, goliyan, woh sab sirf yeh saabit karne ke liye tha ki main galat nahi hoon."

"Aur aapne mujhe kyun nahi bataya?"

Dev ne Kabir ki taraf dekha. Uske chehre par woh halki si muskurahat thi jo Kabir ne pichhle chhe hafton mein shayad teen baar dekhi thi.

"Agar main bata deta, toh tum use dhoondhte, Kabir. Aur jo kisi ko dhoondhta hai, use wahi milta hai jo woh dhoondh raha hai. Main chahta tha tum saboot dhoondho. Naam apne aap aa jaata." Usne ek pal ruk kar kaha, "Aur aaya. Rajpur Road ki us dukaan mein. Tumhare haath mein. Mere nahi."

Kabir kuch der chup raha. Phir usne kulhad wale ladke ko aawaaz di.

"Bhaiya, ek aur chai. Phiki. Bina cheeni."

Dev ne bhauhein uthayin.

"Kya?" Kabir ne kandhe uchkaye. "Kadwi chai, kadwa aadmi. Thoda try kar raha hoon. Shayad isse dimaag tez chalta ho."

"Mujhe meethi hi dena, bhaiya," Dev ne kaha. "Do chammach." Aur pehli baar, chhe hafton mein, woh khul kar hansa.

Usi subah, Silver Pine mein, Pooja ne Kamra Number 7 ka darwaza khola.

Hotel sardiyon ke liye band tha. Chaukhat ki tooti lakdi ki jagah nayi lakdi lag gayi thi, aur brass ki chitkani ka khaana naye screw ke saath wapas apni jagah tha. Palang par safed chadar dhaki thi. Fireplace thandi thi. Desk khaali thi.

Pooja ke haath mein ek chhota sa dabba tha. Payal Thapliyal ne use pichhle hafte bheja tha, ek chitthi ke saath: *Yeh ghadi kabhi evidence thi hi nahi. Ise uski jagah wapas rakh dijiye.*

Pooja ne dabba khola. Brass ki ghadi. Naya sheesha. Aur andar, ek nayi battery.

Usne ghadi ko desk par rakha, theek wahin jahan woh chaudah tareekh ki subah thi, jab usne use 10:15 par ruka dekha tha aur register mein likh diya tha. Phir usne apne phone se time milaya aur suiyan ghumayin.

Ghadi chal padi.

*Tik. Tik. Tik.*

Pooja ne darwaza band kiya, taala lagaya, aur seedhiyan utar gayi.

Khaali kamre mein, band khidki ke paas, dhoop ki ek lakeer desk par aakar ruki. Aur ghadi chalti rahi. Kisi ke liye nahi, bas apne liye. Waqt ginti rahi ek aise kamre mein jahan ab koi nahi tha jo waqt ka hisaab rakhe.

Ghadi phir chal padi thi.

Lekin kuch kamron mein waqt ek baar ruk jaaye, toh phir kabhi poori tarah nahi chalta.
