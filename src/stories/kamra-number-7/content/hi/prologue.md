14 October ki raat Mussoorie mein baarish kuch alag hi thi.

Yeh woh baarish nahi thi jo pahadon par shaam ko aakar chali jaati hai. Yeh ruk-ruk kar nahi, lagataar giri thi. Jaise aasmaan ne kisi purani baat ka hisaab aaj hi chukana ho. Pahadon par chhayi dhund sadak ko nigal rahi thi. Deodar ke pedon ki shaakhein hawa mein ek doosre se takra rahi thin. Aur Camel's Back Road ke aage, ek sunsaan dhalaan par bana purana Silver Pine Hotel kisi bhooli hui yaad ki tarah andhere mein khada tha.

Hotel angrezon ke zamane ka tha. Lakdi ki seedhiyan har kadam par shikayat karti thin, aur corridors mein ab bhi purani kaaleen ki halki si mehak thi. October ka aakhri hissa off-season hota hai. Us raat hotel mein ginti ke log the: maalik Vikram Sethi, unki patni Meera, family doctor Sameer Kapoor, aur Kamra Number 10 mein ek bujurg Negi dampati jo har saal is mausam mein yahan aate the. Vikram ka business partner Arjun Malhotra shaam ki meeting ke baad nikal chuka tha.

Reception par night receptionist Neelam akeli thi.

Yahan kaam karte use teen saal ho gaye the. In teen saalon mein usne seekh liya tha ki purane hotel ki raatein awaazon se bani hoti hain: lakdi ka chatakna, paani ke pipe ki khatkhat, hawa ka khidkiyon se sar takrana. Thodi der pehle basement se generator ki ghurrahat aayi thi, phir kuch minute baad band ho gayi thi. Light do-teen baar jhapki thi. Pahadon mein toofan ki raat itna toh hota hai.

Kareeb 10:54 par manager Rohan Malhotra lobby se guzra. Uske baal geele the aur maroon blazer ke kandhe baarish se gehre ho gaye the.

"Bahadur dada!" usne gate ki taraf aawaz di. "Upar chalo zara. Saat number mein sir phone nahi utha rahe."

Night guard Bahadur apni barsaati jhatakta hua andar aaya, aur dono seedhiyan chadh gaye. Neelam ne zyada dhyan nahi diya. Vikram Sethi ka phone na uthana koi nayi baat nahi thi. Woh aksar kaam mein doobe rehte the, aur jab doobe hote the toh duniya ki parwah nahi karte the.

Woh wapas apne register mein lag gayi.

Raat 11:07 par reception par rakha telephone baja.

Neelam ne receiver uthaya. "Silver Pine Hotel, good evening."

Doosri taraf kuch seconds tak koi kuch nahi bola. Line par sirf ek ajeeb si sarsarahat thi, jaise kahin door baarish kisi tin ki chhat par gir rahi ho. Phir ek aadmi ki ghabrayi hui awaaz aayi:

"Room number seven... jaldi jaiye."

"Ji? Kaun bol rahe hain?"

Line kat gayi.

Neelam kuch pal receiver haath mein liye khadi rahi. Awaaz mein kuch tha jo use achha nahi laga. Woh mazaak karne wale ki awaaz nahi thi. Woh kisi aise aadmi ki awaaz thi jo kisi cheez se bhaag raha ho.

Usne socha, shayad koi guest nashe mein hai. Shayad koi prank hai. Usne receiver rakha hi tha ki upar se ek cheekh sunai di.

Woh cheekh doosri manzil se aayi thi. Lambi, toot-ti hui. Kisi aurat ki.

Neelam ka haath apne aap counter par rakhi ghanti par chala gaya, jaise koi ghanti is waqt kuch theek kar degi.

Ek minute baad seedhiyon par jooton ki tez awaaz aayi. Rohan Malhotra bhaagta hua neeche aaya. Uska chehra safed pad gaya tha. Kandhe ab bhi geele the, aur jab woh counter ke paas aakar ruka toh Neelam ko diesel ki halki si boo aayi, wahi boo jo basement se aati thi.

"Neelam... police ko phone karo. Ambulance bhi." Uski saans phool rahi thi. "Room seven mein Vikram sir... woh—"

Woh apni baat poori nahi kar paya.

"Abhi ek phone aaya tha," Neelam ne dheere se kaha. "Kisi ne kaha room seven mein jaldi jaiye."

Rohan ne uski taraf aise dekha jaise usne koi doosri bhasha boli ho.

"Kisne?"

"Pata nahi. Naam nahi bataya."

Rohan kuch der chup raha. Phir usne khud receiver uthaya aur Mussoorie thane ka number milane laga. Uski ungliyan kaanp rahi thin.

Neeche, Mussoorie ki sadkon par toofan barasta raha. Kolhukhet ke aage ek pahad ka hissa phisal kar sadak par aa gaya, aur Dehradun ka raasta raat bhar ke liye band ho gaya. Jo log us raat Silver Pine mein the, woh ab subah tak wahin the, ek laash ke saath aur ek doosre ke saath.

Kisi ko nahi pata tha ki murder hua kaise.

Aur kisi ko yeh bhi nahi pata tha ki asli raaz Vikram ki maut mein nahi, balki us zindagi mein chhupa tha jise woh bahut pehle peeche chhod aaya tha.
