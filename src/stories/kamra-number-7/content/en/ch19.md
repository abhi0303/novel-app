The next night, at exactly ten o'clock, a strange piece of theatre took place at Silver Pine.

In the kitchen, Thapa made tea in the pot. Plain. No sugar. The same way, in the same pot, on the same tray. Mohan laid the tray: cup, saucer, pot. Dev stood at the kitchen door with a stopwatch in his hand. Kabir followed Mohan with his notebook. Rawat waited on the stairs.

"Exactly the same, Mohan," said Dev. "As that night. Every step. Every stop."
