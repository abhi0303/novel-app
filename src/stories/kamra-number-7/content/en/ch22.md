Rohan's face changed.

"Address: Rajpur Road, Dehradun. Mother's name: Late Smt. S. Malhotra. Father's name: blank." He closed the file. "Sunita Malhotra died on the fourteenth of October, 2024. Meera said softly, "A barsi..."

When he spoke, his voice had changed. The manager's politeness was gone. This was the voice of a thirty-year-old young man, very tired.

"My mother's name was Sunita Malhotra," he said. "She taught at a government school in Dehradun. In 1995 she fell in love with a boy. A small-time contractor with big dreams. Vikram Sethi."
