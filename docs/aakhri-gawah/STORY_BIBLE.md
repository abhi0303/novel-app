# ⚠️ SPOILERS — Aakhri Gawah: Story Bible & Continuity Ledger

> For authors and editors only. Not bundled into the app.

## 1. The truth in one paragraph

**Rahul Sethi (17)** killed his father, **Rajiv Sethi (49)**, at about **7:52–7:54 PM on Friday, 5 December**,
in the study of the family bungalow in Vipul Khand, Gomti Nagar, Lucknow. He struck him twice with the
heavy brass "Samaj Ratna" trophy Rajiv had received for the Pari Sethi Foundation. The second blow came
after a pause, while the doorbell was ringing, and was a choice. Earlier that day Rajiv had used Pari's
memory on a stage again. Nine days before, Rahul had resisted him for the first time (the chain snapped). That evening
Rajiv told him, "Pari kamzor thi. Tum bhi wahi nikle." Rahul wiped the trophy, turned Pari's photograph face
down, took Mohan's salary envelope (₹10,000) to stage a robbery, washed his hands at the garden tap, left by
the back gate (his own key), dumped his bloodied grey hoodie in a riverfront bin, and came home "from
Aditya's" at 9:25 PM. The next morning he asked his friend Aditya to cover for him.

Inspector Dev Rathore hears Rahul's full confession on 14 December and **does not report it**. Nandita, who
had been wrongly arrested, is released through a proper evidence review. The case is eventually closed as
"untraced". Sharda Devi (Nani) reveals that Pari begged her for help three weeks before she died and that she
believed Rajiv's promise. Dev, holding Pari's notebook, recognises his own silence in hers.

**House:** Front door with a video doorbell (cloud events; app on Rajiv's phone). Ground floor: living room; the
study at the rear left (glass-topped teak desk, bookshelf with a silver frame of Pari, brass trophy on the desk);
kitchen at the rear opening to the back yard; Mohan's quarter in the yard; a garden tap by the back gate. The back
gate (padlocked) opens onto the municipal green belt, which leads to the riverfront road (about 10 minutes on foot).
Back-gate keys: Mohan, Rajiv's key ring, a hook in the kitchen, and Rahul's bicycle key. Nandita's house bunch
also has one. Lane-entry CCTV (residents' association) covers the lane only at the main-road end.

**The chain:** heavy gold chain with a round medallion engraved like a sun (Rajiv's father's).
- **The ritual:** before a punishment Rajiv took off the chain and set it on the glass desk. The *clink* meant it was coming.
- **The oath:** "Is chain ki kasam — ghar ki baat ghar mein."
- **26 Nov:** Rahul grabbed it as Rajiv raised his hand and the clasp snapped. It fell on the floor. Rahul's wrist was swollen. Rajiv went quiet for days.
- **27 Nov:** Rajiv sends it via Mohan to Ratan Jewellers for repair. The jeweller notes the clasp was "wrenched, not worn".

## 5. The ₹10,000

- **Where:** a white envelope marked "Mohan — Dec" in Rajiv's hand, top-right drawer of the study desk.
- **Last seen:** 6:25 PM by Mohan.
- **Who knew it existed:** Rajiv, Mohan, Nandita, Rahul (Rajiv made Mohan collect it in the study every 5th, in front of the children), and probably Vivek (he once saw Rajiv pay Mohan).
- **Taken:** by Rahul at about 8:00 PM to stage a robbery. The Rolex, the wallet (₹4,300 and cards), the gold cufflinks and the chequebook were all left behind.
- **What it means as a clue:** the robber knew exactly what to take, so it was an insider. That fits Mohan (his own salary), Nandita (staging) and Rahul alike, and proves none of them guilty.
- **Resolution:** Rahul never opens the envelope. He hands it to Dev during the confession. Dev keeps it unlogged and pays Mohan from his own pocket.

## 8. Dev's choice — consequences

- Withholding the confession is a serious breach. Kabir knows.
- Nandita's release goes through evidence review and procedure, not through Dev's preference.
- The DCP opens an inquiry into the wrongful arrest. Dev takes responsibility, is moved off the case and gets an adverse entry.
- Kabir requests a transfer and does not report Dev, which is itself a weight on him.
- The case is closed "untraced". The envelope and Pari's last page lie unlogged in Dev's drawer. Rahul lives with it.
- Sharda's last page, "Nani ne kaha Papa promise karenge. Main wait karungi. — 19/11", is removed by Dev before Rahul gets the notebook. Dev repeats Sharda's kind of silence and knows it.

## 9. Pari's notebook (spiral, crayon drawings)

- Cover page: "Property of PARI SETHI, Class 6-B. Agar mile toh lauta dena, warna Rahu-Ketu ko bata dungi."
- The drawing: a girl in a pink frock beside flowers and a sun with a face. "I want to be a teacher when I grow up ♥".
- "Things I'll do when I'm big": three dogs; mango ice-cream for dinner; teach in Nani's school; never make a child stand in the corner; buy Rahu-Ketu a cricket bat because Papa broke his.
- "Rahu-Ketu's annoying habits": snores like a pressure cooker; steals my Maggi; says 'Pari tu pagal hai' 100 times a day. Underneath: "But best bhaiya. Don't tell him."
- Three dots drawn in a row: "Our code. Thak thak thak = main theek hoon."
- Gajar the bunny's "biodata".
- The last written page: "Nani ne kaha Papa promise karenge. Main wait karungi. — 19/11"

## 10. Endgame details (as written in Chapters 14–16)

- **The knock:** after the confession, Rahul knocks three times on the barsati wall; Dev knocks back on the stairwell. Sharda hears it, and that is why she trusts Dev. From then on she knocks back at night from her side of Pari's wall, and Rahul knows it is her.
- **Sharda's account (Ch15):** 19 Nov (a Sunday), gajar ka halwa; Pari: "Nani, mujhe apne paas rakh lo… Main roungi bhi nahi." Next morning Rajiv weeps, promises "on Anjali's name", then threatens custody and "never see them again". Three more Sundays (26, 3, 10); she never asks. At the cremation Rajiv says: "Exam pressure… You still have Rahul. Sundays."
- **What Dev does with the truth:** Rajiv was *not* lying when he told Rahul that Nani knew. Dev cuts out the last page with a blade and picks the paper teeth out of the spiral. To Rahul's "Is this all of it?" he says "That's what your Nani had." To "Was he lying about Nani?" he says "Your Nani loves you more than anyone alive."
- **Mohan:** in Barabanki, Dev gives him ₹10,000 in a brown envelope with new notes. "Sahab's envelope was always white." Mohan does not ask. The money goes to Guddi's fees.
- **Kabir:** Sana reports Ayaan to Childline (1098); in January the CWC orders counselling and supervision. Kabir and Sana marry on 12 Feb, and Dev stands at the back.
- **March:** Inspector Rawat's final report (untraced) is accepted; Nandita is discharged; the foundation cancels "Suno". In Dev's drawer: the blue medal box, the sealed white envelope and the folded last page.
- **Last line:** "It is never the first witness, Dev thought, who decides what the world will hear. It is always the last."
