## 1. The truth in one paragraph

On the night of **Saturday 14 September 2019**, the last night of Project Smriti's fourth cohort, **Meera Sanyal (24)**
was the Director's unregistered guest in Room 7. She had been given **zolpidem 10 mg** at 21:30 under an unapproved
"Sleep Consolidation Arm" that Dr Ira Sen ran without ethics approval or proper consent. Meera had already
**sleepwalked** three times (29 Aug, 6 Sep, 11 Sep). The staff logged it and nobody told her. At 23:05 a storm cut the
mains. The generator failed to auto-start (**three crank attempts = three thumps**), and the magnetic lock on the
east service door, **the red door**, released (fail-safe). After a lantern-lit gathering (the **photograph, 23:32**),
Meera went back to bed, and at about **23:40 walked out through the red door in her sleep** and east along the old
sanatorium **cure-walk**, a cliff path closed since 2 Sep because its retaining wall was bulging.

**Ananya Rao (24)**, known in the programme only as **"Gauraiya"**, was Meera's neighbour in Room 6. She had stopped
taking her own pill so that she could keep watch. She heard the door, took her torch and raincoat, and went after her.
At the barricade she turned Meera round and lit her way back towards the door, standing on the outer edge. **At about
23:45 the wall collapsed under Ananya** and she fell into the gorge above the Bhagirathi. **Meera, still asleep, walked
back, pressed her hand to the door's glass (seeing the "EXIT" sticker backwards), went in and closed the door**, as
Ananya had told her to. At 23:47 the technician **Dev Malik** cranked the generator by hand (**three cranks**). At 23:48
the power and the maglock came back, and the door was locked.

At 06:10 the nurse found Meera with both soles cut, mud to the ankles, soaked and with one slipper missing. Ira hid
everything. Meera was sent home believing she had "slept through it" and that Gauraiya had "flown off into her own
sky". Ananya was recorded as having **left voluntarily**, and her body was never found.

- **Twist One (Ch12):** Ananya never left. She died on the cure-walk beyond the red door, and Meera closed that door.
- **Twist Two (Ch15):** Meera was the one who went out. Ananya went after her and died bringing her back. Meera closed
  the door because Ananya told her to.

## 3. Place: Himgiri Institute for Memory & Recovery (HIMR)

The building was a 1931 mission TB sanatorium (St. Luke's) on **Kothiyal Dhar**, a spur about 4 km above Dunda in Uttarkashi
district, with the Bhagirathi gorge to the south. It became HIMR in 2016 and closed in March 2020. Ganga View Resorts
bought it in 2025, and **demolition begins Tuesday 23 Sept 2026**.

**Upper (main) floor**, entered from the north gate:
- **Centre:** entrance hall. West of the hall: the admin office and the Director's office (steel cabinet). South of the hall: the **common room**, with big valley windows and a battery wall clock whose glass is **cracked across the "4"**.
- **West wing:** dining and kitchen (door to the kitchen courtyard), the nurse's **duty room** (landline), and the medical room with its locked pharmacy cupboard.
- **East wing:** a 40 m corridor running east from the hall.
  - North side: Therapy A, the Observation Room (one-way mirror into Therapy A), Therapy B (voice letters were recorded here), the records room and the linen store.
  - South (valley) side: **Rooms 1–7**. Room 6 is Ananya's, and **Room 7 is the last room, about 2 m from the red door**.
- **The red door:** a steel service door with a **push bar** inside and a wired-glass vision panel at head height. An **inside sticker reads "EMERGENCY EXIT — PUSH BAR TO OPEN"** in red letters. The inside face is **cream** like the corridor; the **outside face is fire-red**.
  - It has a **magnetic lock** on the mains board, which is **fail-safe: it releases when the power fails**. It **opens outward** onto an iron landing.
  - The emergency lamp above it had a dead battery from 19 Sep 2019 (log).
  - **The official 2016 plan shows this end as "STORE (SEALED)", with the external stair "TO BE DISMANTLED".** The stair was never removed; Ira informally let Dev use the door as his short route to the generator.

**Lower floor**, which opens south onto the lower terrace and is reached by a ramp from the kitchen courtyard:
- Laundry, the **old mortuary (now the archive: steel almirahs of registers)**, the pump room, and the electrical room (main panel + **AMF panel**).
- The **DG (generator) room** at the east end of the terrace, directly below Room 7 and the landing. Its window looks up at the stair and the cure-walk.

**Outside:**
- **External iron stair:** 22 steps from the landing down to the lower terrace beside the DG room.
- **Cure-walk:** a 1.2 m stone path running east from the landing along the cliff, with a parapet and railing on the valley side. It curves north round the nose of the spur to meet the jeep road below the gate, about 400 m in all.
  - 2 Sep 2019 log: "Cure-walk 35–45 m from landing: retaining wall bulging, seepage — path closed — barricade tape."
  - **About 12 m of wall and path collapsed at ~23:45 on 14/9.**
- **Slippers:** every room's rubber slippers are institute-issued, blue, with the **room number painted in white on the heel**.

## 7. The cassette: content, custody, label

- **Object:** a TDK D60 with an institute sticker "HIMR · VOICE LETTERS". In blue gel, Ananya wrote "**Meera ke liye — G.**"; below it, in black ballpoint, **"7.9.19" in Ira's hand** (her full-stop dates and looped 9). The recorder is the institute's battery Philips, painted "HIMR 3".
- **Session 1 — Sat 7 Sep 2019, ~15:00, Therapy B** (birdsong, a jeep horn):
  - Opens: "*Meera, agar tum ye recording sun rahi ho, toh iska matlab hai ki tumhe abhi tak sach nahi bataya gaya… Main Ananya hoon. Tumhari behen.*"
  - Then: her name ("the promise"); Secunderabad; her mother; the fire and the jammed fire door; why "Gauraiya"; "*Delhi mein ek insaan*" (no name); the night pills and the register "they don't show us".
  - Ends: "*Aur ab woh baat jo koi tumhe nahi bata raha. Meera, tum raat ko—*" **[cut]**.
- **Session 2 — Sat 14 Sep, 22:52–23:07, Room 6** (rain on the tin roof):
  - She deliberately recorded over the blunt sentence: she decided to tell Meera face to face, saying "*kuch baatein tape pe nahi, aankhon mein kehni chahiye*" (some things should be said face to face, not on tape).
  - "*Kal last day hai… maine aaj goli nahi khaayi. Main jaag rahi hoon. Tumhara darwaza abhi tak band hai — main sun sakti hoon. Tum so jao, Rajkumari.*"
  - Then: the fan winding down, **three thumps** (23:05), "*Arre — light gayi*", Annamma's distant "*Sab common room mein aa jao, torch lekar*", stop.
- **Custody:** Room 6 bed (06:00, 15/9) → Annamma → Ira (evening). Ira listened only to the opening, couldn't go on, labelled it from the session register and put it in an envelope (her hand: "*Cohort 4 — 14/15.9 — G. / M. — personal*") with the photo print. **It stayed in the Director's office cabinet when HIMR closed (2020)**: "I couldn't destroy her voice and I couldn't live with it. I left her where she was."
- **Delivery:** Dev forced the cabinet during clearance (late Aug 2026), heard "Meera", recognised her in the photo, found her clinic online, and posted it on 13 Sep from Dehradun with the note: "*Tum us raat wahan thi. Tumne hi darwaza band kiya tha.*"

## 11. Why the investigation failed

The police were shown the 2016 plan with no east exit. Statements were typed from Ira's wording, and the night register
was rewritten. The key witness (Meera) had been removed before police came. The report came 12 hours late. Ananya's
stated wish to leave and Kabir's letters made the police think of elopement. The guard was sheltering from the rain. The
collapse was put down to the monsoon, during a week of landslides across the district. The scree and river were never
searched. The family were embarrassed and pressed the "boyfriend" line.
