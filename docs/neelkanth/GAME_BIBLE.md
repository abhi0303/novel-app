# Neelkanth Dweep: game bible

> ⚠️ Full solution. Read this only if you are writing or changing the case.

## The premise

Neelkanth Dweep is a private island 7 km off Malvan, on the Konkan coast. The first monsoon storm cuts it off from Saturday 13 June 2026 until Monday 15 June at noon. The caretakers left on Saturday morning, so exactly seven people are on the island.

Sunday 14 June is the twentieth anniversary of the sinking of the trawler M.V. *Neelkanth*. Madhav Kamat named the island after the boat.

## What happened in 2006

The *Neelkanth*'s fitness certificate expired in March 2006. The Commander warned Madhav in writing about the bilge pump and hull. Madhav put the survey off until after the monsoon. On 14 June 2006 he ordered Anant to take her to Goa for a cheap dry-dock slot. Anant refused because of the weather. Madhav threatened to give the boat and the crew to another skipper, so Anant went to protect his men. The pump failed off the Vengurla rocks. Four men drowned: Anant Kamat, Joseph Fernandes, Santosh Tandel and Ibrahim Mulla. Prakash Kubal and Dinesh Hodawadekar survived.

That night Madhav called Farida. She got Naik to put a February date on a renewal so the insurance would pay, and coached the two survivors to say Anant had been drinking, so the blame fell on the dead skipper and not the owner.

## The poison

*Cerbera odollam* (othalanga, the "suicide tree") contains cerberin, a cardiac glycoside that acts like digoxin. Vomiting starts 1–2 hours after ingestion, and death follows in 3–6 hours from heart block. In a digoxin patient it passes for digoxin toxicity or a heart attack. The giveaway is that his digoxin strip count is exactly right.

## Structure and choices

Five acts. Each act start is a checkpoint (`a1-player`, `a2-scream`, `a3-start`, `a4-start`, `a5-start`).

### Act 1, Arrival
- **The afternoon walk**: the jetty (gives `o-kozhikode`, `o-three-fruits`) or the lighthouse (gives `o-argument`).
- **Dinner** always gives `o-thumb`.
- **After dinner**: the porch with Nandini (her alibi), the radio with the Commander (his alibi), or your room (`o-footsteps`).

### Act 2, The Body
- **Study investigation**: the body must be examined. The other spots are optional: glass `e-glass`, spoon, pills, typewriter (`e-cover` + `e-ribbon`), drawer `e-medical`, safe `e-safe`, door `e-verandah`.
- **Announce poison?** Yes sets `announced` and gives `t-tara-tasted`, but Farida then gets rid of her sandals. No keeps the sandals in her room.
- **Study key**: keep it (`studyLocked`, which protects the ribbon), give it to the Commander (`keyCommander`, after which Farida borrows it and the ribbon goes), or leave it open (the ribbon goes).
- **Radio**: asking the Commander sets `trustCommander`, so he confesses 2006 in Act 4. Doing it yourself has no effect. Letting Sid do it is flavour only.

### Act 3, The Rooms (hub)
Kitchen, jetty, lighthouse and the study revisit are each available once. **Only three of the five bedrooms** can be searched. Interviews are unlimited.
- Kitchen: `e-mortar`, `e-knife`, the fridge (rules out the jug and the curry), and the bamba (`e-ash`).
- Jetty: the tree `e-tree` (plus `o-two-fruits` if you counted three on Saturday), the lantern box (`e-knife-found`, the knife with sap and blood), the boathouse fibres, and the sand path.
- Lighthouse: `e-logbook`.
- Study revisit: only what you missed, plus the typewriter variant set by the key choice (`o-ribbon-gone` if the ribbon was taken).
- Rooms: Sid (`e-cash`, `e-threats`, `e-will-mail`), Nandini (`e-divorce`, `e-green`), Tara (`e-crew-photo`, `e-tara-notes`, and her bag, which sets `taraBagChecked`), the Commander (`e-navy-book`, `e-share`, `e-radio-notes`), Farida (`e-sandals` or `o-sandals-gone`, `e-elevation`, `e-first-aid`).
- Evidence-gated questions: Nandini/mortar gives `t-mortar-time`. Sid/safe (needs `e-cash` or `e-safe`) gives `t-sid-sighting` and `r-sid-2`. Tara/night gives `t-tara-voices`. Farida/thumb gives `o-cut`.

### Act 4, The Second Night
- If you have the ribbon, or kept the key (you fetch it now), you read it and get `r-confession`.
- If `trustCommander` or you have `e-share`, he tells you about 2006: `r-commander-2006`.
- **The watch**:
  - Guarding Tara's door: you catch Farida with the cut fruit (`t-night-visit`, `e-husk`).
  - Guarding the study: you see a torch (`o-torch`). At breakfast the fruit turns up in Tara's bag.
  - Sleeping: the fruit turns up in Tara's bag at breakfast.
  - In either of the last two cases, if you searched Tara's bag in Act 3 you can prove it was planted (`t-plant-proof`). Otherwise Tara looks guilty.

### Act 5, The Accusation
The player picks who, how and why, or names nobody.

## How the ending is decided (`accusation.judge`)

1. Nobody named → **Heart failure** (`end-none`).
2. Anyone but Farida → **The wrong hands: <name>** (`end-tara`, `end-sid`, `end-nandini`, `end-commander`).
3. Farida, but the method is not *othalanga seed in his solkadhi* or the motive is not *to stop his confession* → **Not proven**.
4. Farida with the right method and motive also needs proof of all three:
   - **Method**: `e-glass` AND (`e-mortar` OR `e-tree`).
   - **Motive**: `r-confession` OR `r-commander-2006` OR (`e-ash` AND `e-logbook`).
   - **Link to her**: `e-sandals` OR `t-sid-sighting` OR `t-night-visit` OR `t-plant-proof` OR (`e-knife-found` AND `o-cut`).
   - All three → **The truth, and justice** (`end-justice`). Otherwise → **Not proven**.

Only `end-justice` reveals the truth, through Farida's confession and the epilogue at the Malvan memorial fourteen months later. The other six endings never confirm who the killer was, and the wrong-accusation endings deliberately do not single out Farida.

## Red herrings

- **Tara**: revenge (her father drowned), and she made and tasted the drink. The fruit planted in her bag points at her.
- **Sid**: debts, the stolen cash, the will email, and his lie about where he was.
- **Nandini**: the divorce papers, the old will, and the mortar is "hers". She was on the porch with the player if they chose it, and she owns nothing white.
- **The Commander**: the poison manual (he looked it up on Sunday morning), the 5% share, and knowledge of the tree. His radio alibi is in the Coast Guard log, and his knee means he cannot move quickly.
