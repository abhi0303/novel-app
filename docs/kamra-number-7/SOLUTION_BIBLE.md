## 1. The answer in one paragraph

**Rohan Malhotra**, the hotel manager, killed Vikram Sethi. He acted alone. Rohan is Vikram's
abandoned son. Vikram left Rohan's mother, **Sunita Malhotra**, at Dehradun railway station in
1995 and never came back. In 2024 Sunita needed heart surgery and wrote to Vikram for help.
Vikram read the letter and did nothing. Sunita died on **14 October 2024**. On the night of
**14 October** (her second death anniversary), Rohan crushed tablets from his mother's leftover
heart medicine and stirred them into Vikram's 10:05 tea. He did it in the first-floor service
pantry, after sending the waiter Mohan to fetch a sugar-free sachet. The poison worked slowly.
Vikram bolted his own door at 10:36 and died alone at about 10:45–10:50. That is the whole
"locked room": the killer never had to go in or come out.

The cruel irony is that Vikram spent his last hour writing Rohan a letter. In it he admitted
everything and promised to name Rohan as his son and heir the next morning. Rohan only read
that letter after he had pulled it from his dead father's fist.

## 3. Backstory

- **1995, Dehradun:** Vikram (27, a struggling contractor) loves Sunita Malhotra (24, a
  schoolteacher). She is pregnant. Vikram gets a chance to marry Kamini, a Delhi industrialist's
  daughter. At Dehradun station he tells Sunita "Do hafte mein lautunga" and boards the Delhi
  train. He never returns.
- **March 1996:** Rohan is born. The birth certificate has no father's name. Sunita raises him
  alone by teaching and tailoring.
- **2010:** Kamini dies. **2018:** Vikram marries Meera.
- **2024:** Sunita needs heart-valve surgery (about ₹8 lakh). She writes to Vikram's Delhi
  office. Vikram reads the letter three times, then locks it in a drawer. He is afraid of scandal
  and of Meera. Sunita dies on **14 Oct 2024**. Before she dies she tells Rohan the truth. Her
  prescribed heart tablets are a cardiac glycoside with a distinctive **pink coating** (a local
  brand). Several strips are left over and Rohan keeps them.
- **May 2025:** Rohan cultivates Arjun Malhotra at a hospitality event, plays up the shared
  surname, and gets a recommendation. Vikram hires him as manager of Silver Pine. Rohan is
  excellent at the job, polite and invisible, and he watches Vikram.
- **Sept 2026:** Vikram prepares to sell Silver Pine. During due diligence he reads the staff
  files. Rohan's file shows: DOB March 1996; mother "Late Smt. S. Malhotra, Dehradun"; father's
  name blank. He also sees Rohan's leave note for the morning of 14 Oct: "barsi". Vikram realises
  who Rohan is.
- **Arjun's crime (unrelated to the murder):** Over two years Arjun siphoned **₹4.2 crore**
  through a fake vendor, "Himgiri Constructions", billed for renovations that never happened. He
  opposed the sale because due diligence would expose him. On 14 Oct at 8:50 PM the accountant
  emails Vikram the audit findings.
- **Meera and Dr. Kapoor (unrelated to the murder):** They have had an affair for two years.
  Vikram has told Meera that he is "changing the will on the 15th"; he has not said in whose
  favour. On 12 Oct Meera phoned Adv. Sharma to ask, and he refused to tell her.

## 5. The key questions, answered

**Cause of death.** A massive overdose of a cardiac glycoside causing fatal arrhythmia. The
drug is the same class as Vikram's own prescription, but the residue in the teapot and cup
carries a **pink tablet-coating dye**. Vikram's tablets and the doctor's sealed bottle are both
white. (The novel never gives doses or a recipe.)

**Time of death.** About 10:45–10:50 PM. The pathologist's window is 10:15 PM to midnight.
Ingestion was about 10:05–10:10; first symptoms by about 10:20.

**The locked room.** It was not a trick. Vikram bolted the door himself after Arjun left (Arjun
heard it). Nobody entered until the forced entry at about 10:58. The bolt plate shows fresh,
genuine splintering. Dev's line in the draft — "We are assuming the killer had to come into the
room and get out again" — is the key: the poison came in with the tea.

**The 10:12 message.** Vikram really sent it, to Arjun, about the ₹4.2 crore fraud.

**The red umbrella at 10:14.** Dr. Kapoor, going to his car for Vikram's new medicine bottle.
The umbrella was an unclaimed lost-and-found item that Rohan had logged on 2 Oct ("entry by:
Rohan M."), which is why the police first read it as "Rohan's umbrella".

**The clock at 10:15.** Its battery died at 10:15 **AM**. It had been stuck all day. Vikram's
fall knocked it over and cracked the glass. Rohan knew it was stuck because of the maintenance
request he ticked, and he built his alibi around 10:10–10:15 in the hope police would take
10:15 as the time of death.

**The wet paper.** A strip of Vikram's letter to Rohan, in Vikram's own handwriting and
fountain-pen ink, on hotel notepad paper with a watermark. It tore when Rohan pulled the letter
from the fist. It is wet with **rainwater carrying diesel traces** from Rohan's hands. The line
"Asli chehra usi ka hai, jisne mujhe aakhri baar zinda dekha" is about **Sunita**, the last
person who saw the "real", living Vikram, at Dehradun station in 1995. It is not an accusation.
It only *sounds* like a dying message. By accident it is also literally true: Rohan was the
last to see his father alive in any meaningful sense, and he was the one who killed him.

**The 11:07 call.** Arjun, from Kandiyal Filling Station, out of guilt for leaving a sick man.

**The scream.** Meera at 11:08, coming from Room 9.

**The bottle.** The doctor's sealed bottle of 30 white tablets, found unopened (30/30) in the
desk drawer. It is a red herring that Rohan deliberately pushed.

**The sugar-free sachet.** A pretext to send Mohan away. Vikram never took sugar or sweetener
(Meera says so). Rohan, as manager, knew that. The sachet was found unopened on the tray.

### Why each suspect lied

| Suspect | The lie | The real reason |
|---|---|---|
| Arjun | "I left at 9:45 and never went to his room." | To hide the fraud, the 10:28–10:36 visit, and the fact that he left a sick man. He also made the 11:07 call. |
| Meera | "I was in my room after 10." | To hide the affair: she was in Room 9 from 10:20 to 11:08. |
| Dr. Kapoor | "Saw him drinking tea at 9:50, normal." | To hide that he really saw Vikram at 10:22, already symptomatic, missed the signs, and then spent the next half hour with Meera. He kept the true detail (the tea) and moved the time, exactly as Dev's maxim predicts. |
| Rohan | "Basement from 10:10, generator inspection began 10:12." / "Saw the doctor take a small bottle to Room 7, around 9:50." | To hide the murder. The bottle remark was meant to frame the doctor, but the bottle was in the doctor's car until 10:15, so Rohan could only have seen it from the 2nd floor at 10:22. **That is his slip.** |

### Decisive evidence against Rohan (all planted before the finale)

1. **The bottle slip** (Ch 7, then Ch 15): Rohan claims he saw the doctor carrying the small
   bottle to Room 7 "around 9:50". The bottle was in the doctor's car until 10:15 (Norbu
   camera), and the doctor went to Room 7 only once, at 10:22. So Rohan was on the 2nd floor at
   10:22, not in the basement.
2. **A dark blazer behind the service-stair glass at 10:22** (Ch 16, Kapoor). Bahadur, the
   only other dark-jacketed staff member, was at the gate at 10:19 (Kapoor saw him) and stayed
   there until 10:54 (Neelam).
3. **The sugar-free pretext** (Ch 2: unopened sachet; Ch 4: Mohan fetched it from the store;
   Ch 9: Meera says Vikram never took sweetener; Ch 19: Mohan admits the tray was alone for a
   minute; finale: the store register reads "order: R.M." and Bahadur says he had already left).
4. **The generator controller log** (Ch 20): the test run was 10:41, not 10:12. The basement
   camera MCB was switched off by hand.
5. **The clock request** (Ch 11 / Ch 20): Rohan ticked "seen", so he knew the clock was stuck at 10:15.
6. **Wet, diesel-stained fragment** (Prologue: Rohan soaked and smelling of diesel; Ch 17: lab
   finds rainwater and diesel). Only Rohan came from the generator and diesel tank.
7. **The torn blister** in the basement oily-waste drum (Ch 20). Batch traced to Doon Medicos,
   Dehradun, and dispensed until Oct 2024 to **Smt. Sunita Malhotra**, who **died 14 Oct 2024**.
   Rohan took leave on the morning of 14 Oct for a barsi (Ch 3), and his file reads "Mother:
   Late Smt. S. Malhotra; father's name blank" (Ch 12).
8. **Physical fit** (finale): the letter hidden behind Sunita's photo in Rohan's cottage is
   missing a strip, and the wet fragment fits it exactly in tear pattern, watermark and ink
   lines. The ESDA impressions from the notepad's next sheet (Ch 17: "…tumhari maa…",
   "…1995…", "…maafi…", "…Sharma ji…") match the letter's text.

## 6. False theories (each reasonable when it is raised)

1. **Arjun alone** (end of Part II, Kabir): he lied about leaving, came back secretly, received
   the threat, and the umbrella man left at 10:14, just before the 10:15 clock. *It breaks
   because* the CCTV figure is unidentified, the clock is unreliable, and nothing explains the
   locked room.
2. **Arjun + Rohan conspiracy** (Ch 12, Kabir and SI Rawat, "the theory everyone believed"):
   the shared surname, Arjun's job recommendation for Rohan, Rohan hiding Arjun's return, the
   master key, and "Rohan's" umbrella. *It breaks because* the bolt plate shows a genuine forced
   entry with no tampering, the assumptions are unproven, and the toxicology changes everything.
3. **Meera + Dr. Kapoor, the lovers** (Ch 14–15): affair, will change, doctor's lies, the
   bottle of the same drug, his time in the room. The strongest theory. *It breaks because* the
   bottle is sealed (30/30), the residue is pink-coated, Vikram was already symptomatic at 10:22
   so ingestion came before the doctor arrived, and Arjun saw him alive and bolting the door at
   10:36.
4. **Suicide or accidental overdose** (Ch 16, briefly): ESDA finds "…maafi…". *It breaks
   because* a suicide note does not vanish on its own, the pink drug is not his brand, and the
   strip in his fist was torn by someone else.
5. **Arjun's revenge** (Ch 18–19, Kabir): "tumhari maa", "1995" and the surname Malhotra
   suggest Vikram wronged Arjun's family. *It breaks because* Arjun's mother is alive in Amritsar,
   and the letter was taken *after* the break-in, when Arjun was 20 km away.
